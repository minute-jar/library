# The operator shares its own content under an editorial account

ProlificLab seeds the public library with banks and learning paths it generates by AI after the official curricula. It shares them under one editorial account, marked as the publisher of the software, never under the personal library accounts of its team. Each such item passes the same review as any other, is read by a person before it is published, and is marked as AI-generated and read. Team members may also share, under their own library accounts, what they made for their own children, as any parent does, without the Selection mark by right. We decided this because content from the company shown under accounts that look like ordinary parents would read as fake community content, which consumer law forbids a business to present, and would expose the team's families.

## Considered Options

- **Team members sharing the seeded content under their own accounts**: rejected, for the reason above.
- **Seeded content without a human reading**: rejected. Wrong answers cost children time, and the editorial account is the operator's own responsibility.

## Consequences

- The curricula serve as a plan of what is taught at which level; the content itself is original, never taken from a textbook.
- English (US Common Core, England's National Curriculum) and French (Éducation nationale) are seeded first, then the other major languages, each once it can be moderated.
