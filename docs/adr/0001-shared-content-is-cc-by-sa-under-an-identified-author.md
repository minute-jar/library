# Shared content is CC BY-SA 4.0, under an author the operator can identify

Everything shared in the public library is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0, one licence for all, attributed to the author's pseudonym and to the free sources cited per question. The author answers for what they share: at their first share they accept the sharing terms, by which they guarantee they hold the rights and that the content is compliant, and the operator, as a hosting provider, can turn to them. To make that possible without exposing a family, a library account needs a verified e-mail and a verified payment card (the subscription's card, or a zero-amount check), and a statement of being an adult and a parent; the operator keeps the identification data the law requires of a hosting provider, and shows only the pseudonym. We decided this because the content reaches other people's children: someone must answer for it, and the content must be free to mix and improve.

## Considered Options

- **CC0**: rejected. No attribution would mean no identity question, but nothing from CC BY or CC BY-SA sources such as Wiktionary could come in.
- **CC BY 4.0**: rejected. Derived works could leave the free commons.
- **A choice of licences per item**: rejected. A parent should not have to choose, and items could not be mixed into one learning path.
- **A restrictive licence for the operator's own seeded content**: rejected. Parents could not derive from it, and a family's export would become ambiguous.
- **E-mail only, or a full identity check**: rejected. An e-mail lets nobody answer for anything; an identity document check would stop almost every parent from sharing. A verified card relies on the bank's check and slows spam.

## Consequences

- Anyone may copy any shared item; the operator does not publish the catalogue in bulk, and its value is curation, moderation and signals.
- Content generated from a school document or made by a child cannot be shared, whatever the parent declares; the origin of every question travels with it (main repository, bank format).
- The card verified for a library account lets billing link it to a subscription; that is written down and never used for anything else.
- The operator is the publisher, not a host, of what it shares under its editorial account (ADR-0002).
